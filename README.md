# feature-spec-sample

A sample feature spec, written the way I'd actually write one for a regulated technical product — problem framing, scoped requirements, compliance considerations, and acceptance criteria a team can build against.

Just to be clear: this isn't a real doc from an employer or client. I wrote it from scratch for my portfolio. The product, the company, the numbers — all invented. The structure and the level of detail are what I use day to day, though, when I'm the one taking a spec from a vague ask to something engineering can actually size and ship.

**[Read the spec](./FEATURE-SPEC.md)**

## Why I'm including this

Most of what I do day to day isn't code. It's turning a fuzzy business requirement into something a cross-functional team can execute against, especially when there's a compliance angle that can't be an afterthought. A spec is usually where that work becomes visible: what's in scope, what's explicitly not, who signs off on what, how we'll know it actually worked.

I picked identity verification / KYC because it's a good stand-in for the kind of work I do most — a technical feature with a hard regulatory constraint, several stakeholders who all care about different things, and a vendor integration sitting in the middle of it.
